# MaatruCare — 6–7 minute judge walkthrough

Use an authorized account containing only fictional demonstration data. Start with an empty workspace if presenting onboarding; otherwise disclose that previously saved fictional examples are present. Do not reset/delete records to prepare the recording.

## Before recording

- [ ] Confirm the approved frontend/backend revisions and healthy authenticated workspace, not only health endpoint success.
- [ ] Verify Google or confirmed email sign-in with the actual authorized account. Local fixtures do not prove live authentication/mail delivery.
- [ ] Confirm simulation capability is allowed for this workspace and labels remain visible. Preserve real caregivers and invitations.
- [ ] Check Gemini with one fictional/de-identified note. If unavailable, demonstrate saved-record questions and explain generation is unavailable; never substitute local test responses in the public app.
- [ ] If invitation email is unconfigured, choose **Create invitation link**, not a claim that email was sent. Use only an approved mailbox/account for real acceptance.
- [ ] Disable desktop notifications that might expose unrelated information. Do not show secrets, provider tokens, database connections or private user data in recording.

## Suggested sequence

1. **0:00–0:50 — Home and Care Pulse.** Explain maternal-care coordination, saved responsibilities, due dates, waiting acknowledgements and actual upcoming visits. Show the current journey phase from saved profile. Say these are coordination priorities, not medical risk predictions.
2. **0:50–1:40 — Care Circle and consent.** Invite a trusted caregiver through the secure link workflow, or add a clearly simulated fictional participant when enabled. Toggle sharing and show the saved state. Explain a real caregiver uses their own matching account; a simulated participant cannot authenticate.
3. **1:40–2:30 — Tasks.** Create a short logistical task, choose an authorized assignee and explicit due date. Complete it and show strikethrough/status. Reopen only if it serves the walkthrough. Counts are derived from saved records.
4. **2:30–3:50 — Handoff and Coordination Story.** Prepare and send a responsibility to the fictional simulated recipient. Use **Acknowledge (simulation)** and **Complete (simulation)**, clearly naming the simulation. Inspect chronological recorded activity, actual authenticated actor and timestamps. If linked to a task, explain its status is independent and is not silently completed.
5. **3:50–4:20 — Timeline.** Show actual saved events, filter Handoffs/Sharing and switch chronology if helpful. Do not imply unlinked tasks and handoffs are connected simply because their titles match.
6. **4:20–5:00 — Lifestyle.** Record a fictional hydration or sleep entry and refresh to show persistence. Describe entered duration and personal goal comparisons, not a medical assessment. Do not claim unrecorded days were missed activity.
7. **5:00–6:30 — AI Care Planner.** Open from Care Pulse. Review authorized local context and an editable minimal brief. Enter a non-sensitive logistical note, explicitly consent to sending it to Gemini, inspect source-backed suggestions and missing facts, choose an assignee/date, approve one task and show it in Tasks/Care Pulse. Clearly distinguish a suggestion from a saved task; no action occurs before approval.
8. **6:30–7:00 — Accountability and limits.** Close on patient agency expressed as “Your care, your choices,” caregiver responsibility and retained history. Describe pilot metrics as measurable future evaluation, not proven clinical outcomes. No clinical validation, compliance certification or real-user impact is claimed without evidence.

## Acceptance checks after recording

- [ ] Refresh retains created fictional records and sharing choices.
- [ ] Sign out; another account sees only its own workspace.
- [ ] No unexpected failed requests/uncaught browser errors occurred.
- [ ] Any real email sent has provider acceptance and separate inbox confirmation. A copied link is not delivered mail.
- [ ] No production cleanup/reset is needed or authorized. Preserve demonstration history.
